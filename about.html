The work behind the title

AI should matter
beyond the lab.

My research explores how artificial intelligence can help us understand meaningful patterns in human behavior. A central focus is supporting earlier, better-informed assessment of neurodevelopmental conditions, including autism.

I work at the intersection of computer vision, multimodal learning, behavioral science and healthcare. The goal is not technology for its own sake—it is research that can eventually help people and the professionals who support them.

Teaching is part of that same mission. Whether in a university classroom, a public talk or a self-paced resource, I want people to understand not only what AI can do, but how it works and when it should be used.
