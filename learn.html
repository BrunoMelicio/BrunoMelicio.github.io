Teaching philosophy

Modern tools change quickly. The principles behind them last.

01

Foundations before shortcuts.

Build an understanding of why a method works before relying on a framework or prompt.

02

Learn by building.

Turn theory into code, experiments and useful systems through purposeful practice.

03

Current tools. Durable understanding.

Use today's best tools while developing knowledge that remains useful when those tools change.
