UGC character foundationFree

Create a photorealistic UGC influencer character photographed for authentic social content. Define a clear, memorable identity: [age range], [appearance], [hair], [personal style] and [personality]. Place the character in [everyday location] doing [natural action]. Use believable phone-camera framing, available light, realistic skin texture, subtle imperfections and an unforced expression. The result should feel captured rather than staged—no beauty-filter skin, exaggerated anatomy, luxury-ad polish, artificial bokeh, text or watermarks. Preserve this exact facial identity and defining features for future scenes.
